Howick
On Main.

A 7,600 m² retail centre with Checkers, Dis-Chem, Ocean Basket, Spur, Mugg & Bean and Mr Price as anchor tenants, delivered by Unicon as construction manager to a design by Visi Architects.

Construction Management JBCC Series 6.2 Retail

Project Overview

What Was Built.

Howick on Main is a new 7,600 m² retail centre in the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands. Its anchor tenants are some of South Africa's best-known national brands, and each brings its own fit-out requirements, opening date and landlord obligations.

Unicon was appointed here as construction manager under JBCC Series 6.2, not as a lump-sum main contractor. In that role Unicon's job is to plan the programme, manage the trade contractors, control cost and quality and keep the centre moving towards a fixed trading date. It is the appointment most often used on retail developments where tenant requirements are still evolving while the structure goes up.

Unicon’s Appointment

Construction Management

Unicon is appointed to plan and manage the trade contractors on the employer's behalf, controlling programme, cost and quality while the employer keeps visibility of every trade price. It suits projects, such as retail developments, where tenant requirements are still developing while the structure goes up.

JBCC Series 6.2 is the Joint Building Contracts Committee Principal Building Agreement, the standard contract for building work in South Africa.

Questions

About This Project.

What is the difference between a full contract and construction management?

On a full contract the contractor holds one agreement with the employer and takes responsibility for delivering the whole building for the agreed price. As construction manager the contractor plans and manages the trade contractors on the employer's behalf, with cost and programme control, which gives the employer more visibility and flexibility while the design is still developing.

Which national brands trade at Howick on Main?

The anchor tenants are Checkers, Dis-Chem, Mr Price, Ocean Basket, Spur and Mugg & Bean.

Does Unicon build shopping centres in KwaZulu-Natal?

Yes. Unicon has delivered retail centres in Howick and Pietermaritzburg, including the 17,000 m² Invesco centre, as well as education and healthcare buildings.

Which JBCC edition was used?

JBCC Series 6.2, the current Joint Building Contracts Committee agreement.
