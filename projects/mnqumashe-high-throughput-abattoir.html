Portfolio / Agricultural EPC / Butterworth, Eastern Cape

Mnqumashe
Abattoir.

A new high throughput cattle and sheep abattoir in the Eastern Cape: demolition of the old building, cut-to-fill platforms, portal-frame steel structures and a full services and mechanical equipment installation.

Full Contract (construction scope) JBCC Series 5 Agricultural

Project Overview

What Was Built.

The Mnqumashe abattoir, in the Mnquma Local Municipality near Butterworth, is a high throughput facility for cattle and sheep. It sits in the R50M+ value band and is among the most complex projects in the Unicon portfolio, because an abattoir is a building and a production line at the same time: floors fall to drains, surfaces are washable, and every service has to land exactly where the equipment needs it.

Unicon carried out the works under JBCC Series 5. The work began with demolition of the existing building and earthworks to form new cut-to-fill platforms, followed by the portal-frame steel structure and the installation of all services and mechanical equipment.

Questions

About This Project.

What makes an abattoir harder to build than a standard industrial building?

Hygiene and process drive everything. Floors must fall to drains, surfaces must be impervious and washable, and the building has to be finished around the equipment, which means civil, steel and mechanical trades are tightly coordinated.

Did Unicon install the mechanical equipment?

Yes. The scope covered all services and the abattoir mechanical equipment.

Does Unicon build agricultural and food-processing facilities?

Yes. Agricultural EPC includes dairies, abattoirs, packhouses and irrigation infrastructure. Mnqumashe sits alongside food-industry work for Nestlé.

What contract form was used?

JBCC Series 5, with Unicon carrying out the full scope of the works.
